**Description.** A chat assistant on a hosted large language model that answers insured persons' questions about coverage and claims, retrieves the policy conditions and, after login, looks up the caller's own claim status through an internal API.

**Intended purpose.** First-line answers to coverage and claim-status questions; hand-off to a human agent for anything else. No decisions on coverage or payment.
