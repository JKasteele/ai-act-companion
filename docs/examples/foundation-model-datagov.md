## 5. Data quality — dimensions and status
| Dimension | Definition | AI Act hook | Status | Suggested metric |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Accuracy | Values correctly describe the real-world object or event. | Art. 10(3): datasets 'free of errors' to the best extent possible. | **Not applicable** | Error rate against a verified reference sample. |
| Completeness | Required values are present; missingness is known and handled. | Art. 10(3): datasets 'complete'; Art. 10(2)(e) data availability. | **Assessed qualitatively (reviewed, no metric)** | Share of records with all mandatory fields populated. |
| Consistency | The same fact is represented the same way across datasets and time. | Art. 10(2)(c) data-preparation operations; Art. 10(4) context of use. | **Assessed qualitatively (reviewed, no metric)** | Cross-source reconciliation failures per 1,000 records. |
| Timeliness | Data is current enough for the decision it feeds. | Art. 10(4): data reflects the specific setting in which the system is used. | **Assessed qualitatively (reviewed, no metric)** | Age of the newest record vs. the refresh SLA. |
| Validity | Values conform to the defined format, type and range. | Art. 10(2)(c) cleaning/labelling; Art. 10(3) 'appropriate statistical properties'. | **Measured (metric, threshold and evidence exist)** | Share of values failing schema or range rules. |
| Uniqueness | No unintended duplicates; one entity is represented once. | Art. 10(2)(f–g): duplicates skew group statistics and bias checks. | **Measured (metric, threshold and evidence exist)** | Duplicate rate after entity resolution. |
| Representativeness & bias screening | The data covers the persons, groups and settings the system will meet. | Art. 10(3) 'sufficiently representative'; Art. 10(2)(f–g) bias examination; Art. 26(4) deployer duty on input data. | **Assessed qualitatively (reviewed, no metric)** | Coverage per (proxy for) protected group vs. the target population. |
